# Brand and editorial contract

Source: PRODUCT.md and live homepage, verified 2026-09-20.

Built with Django is a curated community showcase and learning hub for real products built with Django. It serves builders, learners, makers, and Django hiring teams; it is not an unmoderated general-business promotional directory.

Friendly, practical, independent, community-led. Use current primary sources, real Django behavior and reproducible examples. Never fabricate studies or imply that unrelated listing traffic is qualified Django acquisition. A nontechnical business can still be a legitimate Django project.

Operational priority (inferred from PRODUCT.md): relevant discovery and usefulness before article volume. No new business positioning or revenue target is assumed. Public shipping follows branch, PR, CI and current-head automated review, then merge and live verification. Outreach, social and newsletter sends are outside daily SEO authority.
